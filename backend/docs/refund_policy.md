# Refund policy

## Refund window
Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original packaging. After 30 days, we offer store credit only.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item. Shipping fees are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged or was the wrong item.

## Damaged or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged or is not what was ordered, the customer gets a full refund including shipping, and we send a prepaid return label. Ask the customer for a photo of the damage.
