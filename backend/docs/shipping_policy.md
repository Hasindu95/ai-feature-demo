# Shipping policy

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days. Orders placed after 2 PM ship the next business day.

## Late or lost parcels
If a parcel has not arrived 10 business days after shipping, it is treated as lost. We resend the order for free or issue a full refund, whichever the customer prefers. Always share the tracking number with the customer.

## Changing the delivery address
The address can be changed only before the order ships. Once shipped, the customer must contact the courier directly using the tracking number.
