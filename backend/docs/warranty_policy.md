# Warranty policy

## Warranty period
All electronics carry a 12-month warranty from the delivery date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects, not accidental damage such as drops or water.

## Making a warranty claim
The customer sends the order number and a short video or photo showing the fault. We repair or replace the item within 14 days. If it cannot be repaired, we replace it with the same or a newer model.

## Battery issues
Batteries are covered for 6 months. After 6 months, a battery replacement costs a flat fee of $25.
